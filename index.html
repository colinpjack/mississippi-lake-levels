Dock guidance

  • Expect little or no freeboard on many docks while levels stay elevated.
  • Secure floatables; check lines, chains, and shore power.
  • Wait for a sustained multi-day drop before major dock reconfiguration.

Boat & swimming

  • Go slow near shore; watch for debris; no wake at docks.
  • Supervise kids/pets; avoid current near inlets, bridges, and dam approaches.
